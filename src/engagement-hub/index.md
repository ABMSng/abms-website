## 2026 Webinar Series

**Webinar 1 (September 2026)**
*Beyond South Africa: Xenophobia, African Migration and the Future of Pan-Africanism*
Examines whether xenophobia is becoming a continental phenomenon, the rise of anti-migrant nationalism across Africa, and implications for Pan-Africanism and the African Union's free movement agenda.

**Webinar 2 (October 2026)**
*Porous Borders, Regional Insecurity and Border Walls: Can Physical Barriers Defeat Insurgency in the Sahel and Lake Chad Basin?*
Examines how porous borders facilitate insurgency and organized crime, and whether border walls or stronger regional cooperation offer more sustainable security solutions.

**Webinar 3 (November 2026)**
*Free Movement in East Africa: Lessons for Regional Integration and Continental Mobility*
Evaluates the East African Community's experience with free movement of people, goods, labour, and services, and what it offers for the African Union's Agenda 2063.
