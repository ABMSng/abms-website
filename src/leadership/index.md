# Leadership

## Convener / Initiator

**Samuel Kehinde Okunade, Ph.D.**

This proposed society is the brainchild of Dr. Samuel Okunade of the Department of Border and Migration Studies, Lagos State University, Ojo, Lagos State, Nigeria.

## Directors

**Samuel Kehinde Okunade, Ph.D.** — *Director of Research and Knowledge Exchange*
Oversees research programmes, collaborative projects, journal publications, policy briefs, and the association's research agenda.

**Leon Mwamba Tshimpaka, Ph.D.** — *Director of Training, Conferences and Professional Development*
Coordinates conferences, workshops, webinars, certification programmes, mentorship, and capacity-building initiatives for members.

**Lukong Stella Shulika, Ph.D.** — *Director of Partnerships, Policy and Outreach*
Leads engagement with governments, regional organizations (e.g., AU, ECOWAS, SADC), universities, development partners, civil society, and oversees policy advocacy, fundraising, and external relations.
